UNDERSTANDING THE CONDITION

What is Multiple Sclerosis?

Help you or someone you love navigate MS.

🧠 The basics

Multiple sclerosis (MS) is a long term chronic condition of the central nervous system which impacts the brain, spinal cord, and optic nerves. In MS, the immune system mistakenly attacks myelin which is the protective coating around nerve fibers. When myelin is damaged signals between the brain and the body can slow down, become distorted, or stop altogether.

sclerosis means scarring, and multiple refers to the many scars (which is also called lesions or plaques) that can form as the condition progresses. Everyone's MS is different so no two people experience it exactly the same way.

ℹ️ MS is not contagious, and it is not directly inherited but genetics can play a very small role in risk.

🔄 The types of MS

1 of 3

Relapsing Remitting (RRMS)

The most common form (around 85% of diagnoses). Periods of new or worsening symptoms (relapses) are followed by periods of recovery or stability (remission).

Secondary Progressive (SPMS)

Over time, some people transition from relapsing-remitting MS to a stage where symptoms gradually worsen, with or without relapses.

Primary Progressive (PPMS)

A smaller group experience gradually worsening symptoms from the very beginning, without distinct relapses.

🌍 Who does MS affect?

2 of 3

Most people are diagnosed between the ages of 20 to 50. MS is around 2 to 3 times more common in women than in men. It occurs in every region of the world but is more frequently diagnosed higher north or south farther from the equator.

MS isn't considered fatal for most people and many live very full, active lives with the condition especially with today's treatments.

💊 Managing MS

3 of 3

There is currently no cure, but there are more treatment options than ever:

• Disease-modifying therapies (DMTs) can reduce relapses and slow progression.
• Symptom management from physical therapy to medications it helps with daily life.
• Lifestyle movement, rest, ways to cool off,and emotional support matter.

🎗️ A word of hope

♡

An MS diagnosis changes things but it can't define you. Millions of people around the world live meaningful, joyful lives with MS. Progress in research continues every year. tools like tracking your symptoms can give you and your doctors the clarity to face it together.

⚠️ This page is educational and does not replace advice from your neurologist or MS medical support. Always speak with your clnician about your personal situation.
